# Product

## Register

brand

The storefront (nutrigreenz.farm) is the primary surface and is brand register. The operator console (`/admin`, admin.nutrigreenz.farm) is product register; treat any task on it as product.

## Users

**Customers** in Hyderabad buying cold-pressed oils, A2 ghee, honey and rice for their own kitchens. They are paying a premium for food they can trace, so they read labels, lot codes and farm names.

**Operators** run the business from the console, often in the evening:

- **Staff admins** process today's work: orders to confirm and pack, payments to follow up, stock that is running out, captains to allocate.
- **The owner (super admin)** judges how the business is doing: revenue over time, what sells, where customers come from, money collected versus still owed.

The console splits by page along that line. **Overview** answers the staff question, "what needs action now". **Reports** and **Analytics** answer the owner question, "how is the business doing".

## Product Purpose

A farm-to-table store for single-origin staples, where every claim is traceable to a farm, a batch and a lab report. Success on the storefront is trust that converts. Success in the console is an operator who knows what to do next, and an owner who can see the trend without exporting a spreadsheet.

## Brand Personality

Premium, natural, traceable. Warm and unhurried on the storefront; calm, exact and trustworthy in the console. The palette is forest, cream and gold; the brand has no blue.

## Anti-references

- **Crypto or trading screens:** neon on black, glowing lines, dense tickers. Revenue is not a price feed.
- **Spreadsheets:** tables of numbers with no hierarchy, no comparison and no picture of the trend.
- **Unevidenced marketing numbers:** "40+ tests", "5000+ families". Every figure shown must be one the business can produce a document for.
- A cold, blue SaaS look anywhere in the product; a dark storefront.

## Design Principles

1. **Answer the page's question first.** Each screen leads with the one thing its reader came for; everything else is supporting detail.
2. **Honest numbers.** Every figure comes from real data. No invented deltas, no placeholder trends; an empty state says why it is empty.
3. **Compare, don't just count.** A number is shown with the context that makes it judgeable: previous period, share of total, target.
4. **Quiet at rest, exact on demand.** The resting view stays calm; hover and focus reveal precise values.
5. **Suit the room.** The console is a tool used day and evening, so both themes are first-class and colour carries meaning, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Component boundaries meet 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) in both console themes; text meets 4.5:1. Charts never rely on colour alone: values are labelled or reachable by keyboard focus. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes movement but keeps state changes visible.
