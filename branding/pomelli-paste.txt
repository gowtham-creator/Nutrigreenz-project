Nutri Greenz is a Hyderabad-based wholefoods brand selling single-origin, farm-traceable Indian staples — cold-pressed oils, A2 bilona ghee, raw forest honey, millets and spices. We sell food exactly as nature makes it: cold-pressed below 40°C, hand-churned bilona ghee, raw unheated honey — and every batch is traceable from its lot code back to the exact farm or cow.

Our customers are health-conscious urban Indian households aged 28–45 who want proof, not marketing claims, and will pay more for verified provenance and clean labels.

Brand voice: warm, grounded and plain-spoken — confident without hype, always led by the farm and the method. Tagline: "Born in Soil, built for tomorrow."

Visual identity: earthy and premium. Core colours are deep forest green (#14331F), gold/clay (#B5862F), warm amber (#F29E12) and cream (#F5F2E0). Display type is Roboto Slab (serif), with a leaf-and-"N" logo and botanical, hand-harvest imagery on cream paper.

Flagship products: Cold-Pressed Groundnut Oil (1L), A2 Gir Cow Bilona Ghee (500ml) and Wild Forest Honey (500g).
