## 4) Getting the LOGO + TEXT crisp (important)
AI image models — Seedream included — still **mangle small label text and logos** (you saw this in Pomelli). Two reliable routes:

- **A · Reference-image route** — attach the real Nutri Greenz logo PNG as a reference and instruct *“use this exact logo on the label, keep it sharp, do not invent text.”* Best brand fidelity Seedream can do natively.
- **B · Blank-label route (most reliable)** — generate the jar with a **plain blank cream label**, then **composite the real Nutri Greenz label/logo on top afterward** (Photoshop / Canva — or with the curved-warp + lighting pipeline we already used). This guarantees 100% correct, razor-sharp branding.

**Rule of thumb:** keep on-label words to a minimum in the prompt (wordmark + one line). Fewer words = fewer garbles.
