## 1) MOTION PROMPT (paste into Seedance)

A cinematic 10-second premium product film for **Nutri Greenz A2 Bilona Ghee**.

**Open on the first frame** — a clear glass jar of glossy golden A2 ghee on a rustic wooden table, a wooden spoon with a slow ghee drip, wheat ears and fresh green leaves, soft green backdrop. Begin a **slow, smooth dolly push-in** toward the jar. The **golden ghee glistens** and catches the warm light; the **drop of ghee on the spoon swells and slowly drips** with a tiny ripple; the **wheat sways gently**; fine **dust and light particles drift** through a warm sunbeam. Shallow depth of field; the label **“A2 BILONA GHEE · 100% Pure”** eases into crisp focus. Motion is gentle, appetising, luxurious and lifelike — **keep the jar, label text and logo perfectly sharp and unaltered, no warping**.

**In the final 2 seconds**, the scene gracefully **dissolves and light-blooms into a clean white frame** that reveals the **Nutri Greenz logo** (leaf-“N” mark, “NUTRI GREENZ” wordmark and the tagline “Born in Soil, built for tomorrow”) — the last frame — with a **subtle shimmer on the green leaf**.

**Camera:** smooth cinematic dolly-in, then a soft bloom transition. **Lighting:** warm golden-hour, soft premium highlights. **Style:** high-end commercial food/product advertising, photorealistic, 4K, elegant and clean.

**Negative / avoid:** warped jar or label, garbled text, distorted logo, jitter, morphing, extra objects, harsh flicker, oversaturation.

## 3) Sound design (post)
- **Voice:** Indian Hindi, warm & premium. ElevenLabs (a Hindi/Indian voice) or a real VO artist. Pace: calm, ~3s per line.
- **Music:** soft Indian instrumental — gentle flute/bansuri + light sitar + soft tabla, warm and uplifting, low under the VO.
- **Foley:** subtle ghee “drip”, faint warm-kitchen ambience.
- **Mix:** music −18 dB under VO; small swell on the logo reveal.

## 4) On-screen text (optional, post)
- Over jar: “A2 बिलोना घी · 100% शुद्ध”
- On logo: tagline already in frame — hold 1.5s, end card.

## 5) Tips
- Generate 2–3 takes; pick the one where the **drip + label stay clean** (no morphing).
- If Seedance warps the label, shorten motion (less push-in) or do a 5s cut.
- Keep the **logo end-frame exactly as uploaded** so the brand reveal is razor-sharp.
- Export master at 4K, then cut **9:16**, **1:1** and **16:9** versions for each channel.
