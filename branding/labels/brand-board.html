Who we are

Hyderabad-based wholefoods brand selling single-origin, farm-traceable Indian staples — cold-pressed oils, A2 bilona ghee, raw forest honey, millets & spices. Food exactly as nature makes it: cold-pressed below 40°C, hand-churned ghee, raw unheated honey — every batch traceable from lot code back to the farm. For health-conscious urban households who want proof, not marketing. Voice: warm, grounded, plain-spoken — premium without hype.
