## 1. Business identity
- **Name:** Nutri Greenz (Nutrigreenz Farm Technologies Pvt. Ltd.)
- **Location:** HITEC City, Hyderabad, Telangana 500072
- **Category:** Single-origin, traceable Indian food staples — cold-pressed oils, A2 ghee,
  raw honey, millets, spices, superfoods.
- **One-liner:** Farm-traceable wholefoods, sold exactly as nature made them.
- **Tagline:** *Born in Soil, built for tomorrow.*

## 2. Positioning
Premium-but-honest. We compete with Anveshan / Two Brothers / Pure & Sure on
**traceability** (scan the lot → see the farm/cow), **minimal processing** (cold-pressed
below 40 °C, bilona ghee, raw honey), and **fair farmer pricing**. Not "cheap health
food" — considered, gift-worthy, everyday-premium.

## 3. Audience (ICP)
- Health-conscious urban households, 28–45, metro India (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai).
- Value provenance and clean labels; willing to pay more for proof, not marketing.
- Pain point: "organic" claims they can't verify; oils/ghee/honey adulteration fears.

## 4. Brand voice
Warm, grounded, plain-spoken. Confident without hype. Lead with the farm and the method,
let the proof do the selling. Avoid superlatives and medical claims.
